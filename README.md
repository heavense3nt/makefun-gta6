**VELDORA** is an open-world crime game set in Veldora Bay, a sun-soaked coastal city you explore on foot and by car. You play Kai Mercer, a new driver working off a debt to Mara Solano and her garage, while Vince Kroll's crew tries to run you out of town.

The city has around 180 buildings on a street grid, a beach and sea to the south, traffic and pedestrians, a park, a plaza and a harbor. Steal cars and drive them, build a wanted level from 0 to 5 stars and outrun the Bay PD cruisers that chase you, shoot and fight, and work through 5 story missions — stealing your first wheels, a timed coastal delivery, losing a 2-star heat, a harbor heist against Kroll's rivals, and a final showdown chase against Kroll himself. A HUD with a live minimap, wanted stars, health, cash and speed keeps track of it all, and your progress saves between sessions.

## What is inside

- **Driving** — arcade car physics with acceleration, braking, reversing and speed-sensitive steering; traffic follows the street grid and police cruisers chase, ram and bust you.
- **On foot** — camera-relative movement (D always strafes toward the screen's right), sprint, punching and a hitscan pistol with reloads.
- **Wanted system** — crimes raise your stars; stay clean and out of sight and the heat cools off. Getting busted costs 10% of your cash and sends you back to the garage.
- **Missions and story** — five jobs with objectives, a glowing beacon marker, timers, rival shooters and a final car chase.
- **Sound** — everything is synthesized live with WebAudio: engine hum that follows your speed, sirens, gunshots and a bass loop. No audio files.
- **Save** — cash, mission progress, position and health persist in localStorage.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser. Keyboard and mouse: WASD to move and drive, E to steal or exit a car, mouse click to shoot, F to punch, R to reload, Shift to sprint, drag the mouse to swing the camera, Esc to pause.
