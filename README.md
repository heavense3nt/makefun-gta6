**VELDORA** is an open-world crime game set in Solmar Bay, a sun-soaked coastal city built from scratch for the make.fun GTA 6 bounty. Explore the city on foot or behind the wheel, steal cars, dodge traffic and pedestrians, and climb the wanted ladder from 0 to 5 stars while the Solmar Bay police hunt you down. Run five story missions for the Veldora crew — a first fare, a shady pickup, a stolen sports car, a full-on heat wave, and the Big Score bank job — with pistol combat, street cash to grab, a HUD with a live circular minimap, and a save system that keeps your cash, health and mission progress. Everything is original: the city, the characters, the story, the art and the synthesised WebAudio sound. No files are fetched from the network — the game runs entirely offline from `game/`.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome and click PLAY.

## Controls

- **W A S D** — move on foot / drive (A steers left, D steers right)
- **Mouse drag** — orbit the camera · **Wheel** — zoom · **C** — camera view
- **E** — steal / exit a car · **Space** — jump / brake · **Shift** — sprint
- **Click** — shoot · **R** — reload · **F** — horn · **M** — start the next job
- **P / Esc** — pause and save · **H** — hide the HUD

## What is inside

- A coastal city with ~180 buildings, a beach, a pier and a marina sign
- Traffic and pedestrians with their own routines, plus a day cycle with dusk lamps
- A wanted system (0–5 stars) with police interceptors that chase, ram and bust
- Five missions with a story: Kai Reyes, Mara Voss and the Veldora crew
- Combat, health and armour, street cash pickups, first-aid kits and a save slot
